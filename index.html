About

Quick bites. South Indian delights.

Cafe Rameshwar is a pure vegetarian South Indian cafe in Pant Nagar, Ghatkopar East — dosa, idli, filter coffee, and a kitchen that also runs Jain options. Independent neighbourhood cafe (not The Rameshwaram Cafe chain).

Walk in for breakfast, lunch, or a late filter coffee. Takeaway and delivery are available via the usual apps.
